## 2. Overview

This document describes the procedure for enabling the Automatic Screenshot (*Automatischer Bildschirmdruck*) feature on the HMI of the customer machine.

---

## 3. Prerequisites

- A USB stick assigned the drive letter **G:** must be connected to the machine's HMI before beginning the procedure.

---

## 4. Procedure

> Documented by [Author 1].

1. **Log in** using the username `gronservice2`. The password will be communicated separately.
2. **Navigate** to the path: `Service → Bedientabeau → Inbetriebnahme`.
3. **Click** the **"Fernwartung"** button.
4. **Activate** the **"Display access level"** button.
5. **Activate** the **"Automatischer Bildschirmdruck"** button.

---

## 5. Verification

Once the feature is enabled, the status bar on the HMI will confirm that **"Automatischer Bildschirmdruck"** is active.
